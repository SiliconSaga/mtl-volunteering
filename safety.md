Nothing on this page is optional. Safety is the first of the league's three priorities — before learning, before fun.

## Before the Season

- **Background check** — every coach completes a [simple online form](https://opportunities.averity.com/opportunity/26881); it stays valid for multiple seasons. Your commissioner sends the link.
- **Concussion training** — coaches receive a link to a **required digital training course on concussions**. Complete it before the season starts.

## First Aid

- Every **team bag** includes basic first aid supplies — check yours at pickup and restock at the [League House]({{ site.baseurl }}/league-house/) when anything runs low.
- Arrive early enough to **check the playing surface for hazards** — sticks, rocks, debris, broken glass, standing water.
- **Any injury must be reported** to your sport's league mailbox, same as any other incident — see the [home page]({{ site.baseurl }}/) for contacts.

> **Coordinator input needed:** whether any volunteers hold current first aid / CPR / AED certification per sport, where the nearest AEDs are at each venue, and whether the league sponsors certification courses. If you know, this page wants it.

## Concussions: When in Doubt, Sit Them Out

If a player takes a hit to the head or body and shows **any** sign of concussion — headache, dizziness, confusion, balance problems, vision changes, "just not right" — remove them from play immediately. They do **not** return the same day, and they don't return at all until cleared by a medical professional.

- The CDC's [HEADS UP](https://www.cdc.gov/heads-up/index.html) program is the reference the required training draws on — the [concussion signs and symptoms](https://www.cdc.gov/heads-up/signs-symptoms/index.html) page is worth bookmarking on your phone.
- No game matters more than a child's brain. This call is never wrong to make.

## Weather

- Games are played in the rain, but **thunder, lightning, or unsafe conditions end any game immediately** — don't wait for a league call to clear the field for lightning.
- Cancellation decisions are made at the **league level**, not by individual coaches; the league emails ahead of the session when weather forces a call.

## Emergencies

Call **911 first** for any serious injury. League reporting comes after the child is taken care of.
