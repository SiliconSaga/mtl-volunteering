How equipment flows through the league — from ordering to the field and back.

## Ordering

Gear purchases run through the league so quality and budget stay consistent.

> **Coordinator input needed:** who approves purchases per sport, preferred vendors, and lead times (uniforms especially — they need to arrive before team-bag assembly). If you handle ordering for your sport, please send this page what you know.

## Distribution

- Gear reaches coaches through **team bags** picked up at the [League House]({{ site.baseurl }}/league-house/) before the season.
- **Uniforms are the same across seasons** — remind parents that last season's shorts, socks, and shin guards are reusable, which saves changing at the field and stretches league inventory.
- **Sizing:** ask parents early whether a child runs small or large for their age and arrange the uniform lineup accordingly — it minimizes poor fits and swaps.
- **Only rostered players get uniforms.** No exceptions — rosters in TeamSnap are the source of truth.

## Sharing & Returns

- Extra protective gear (shin guards, and sport-specific items like goalie equipment) may be available beyond the standard age-group provision — ask your commissioner rather than buying out of pocket.
- Anything league-owned goes **back to the League House** at season's end: unclaimed uniforms, unused first aid stock, shared protective gear.

## Field & Rink Gear

Nets, goals, corner flags, cones, and similar shared equipment live at the venues or the League House depending on the sport and season.

> **Coordinator input needed:** per-venue setup/teardown expectations (who puts up nets, where cones live, what stays out overnight) — today this travels by word of mouth; it belongs here.
