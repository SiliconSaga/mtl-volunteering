## Getting In

Access is coordinated per season — your commissioner emails pickup instructions and the door code before the season starts. **Codes are never posted publicly**; if you need access and haven't received instructions, email your sport's contact (see the [home page]({{ site.baseurl }}/)).

## Team Bag Pickup

Coaches pick up a team bag for each team they coach, typically within the **last week or two before the first game**. A team bag contains:

- **Uniforms** — jerseys, shorts, and socks for every player on your roster
- **Shin guards or sport-specific protective extras** where the age group calls for them
- **Basic first aid supplies** — be prepared and ready!
- **Miscellaneous game items** (game ball, markers, etc. — varies by sport)

Return anything unused at the end of the season so the next season starts with a full inventory.

## Supplies

The league keeps shared supplies at the house — first aid restocks, field/rink incidentals, and cleaning supplies.
