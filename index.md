## New Volunteer?

Welcome! No experience is needed for most roles — coaches are usually parents, and the league will set you up with training, gear, and a commissioner to lean on. To get started, reach out to the sport you want to help with:

- **Soccer** — [MTLsoccer@gmail.com](mailto:MTLsoccer@gmail.com) · [soccer.mountaintopleague.com](https://soccer.mountaintopleague.com/)
- **Hockey** — [mountaintop.hockey@gmail.com](mailto:mountaintop.hockey@gmail.com) · [hockey.mountaintopleague.com](https://hockey.mountaintopleague.com/)
- **Everything else** — start at [mountaintopleague.com](https://mountaintopleague.com/)

Coaches complete a **background check** (a simple online form, good for multiple seasons) and required **concussion training** before the season — see [Safety]({{ site.baseurl }}/safety/).

## Per-Sport Details

Rules, schedules, field and rink locations, and season logistics live on each sport's own site — this guide covers what's shared. When in doubt, your sport's commissioner is the answer key.
