MTL runs **recreational programs** — the priority is safety, learning, and fun, in that order. For many kids this is their first season of organized anything. They're here to run around, make friends, and figure out which direction to go.

## Setting the Tone

Coaches set the tone, and parents amplify it:

- **Cheer for effort**, not just goals — "great hustle!" matters more than the score
- **Encourage all the kids**, not just your own — a kind word from the sideline goes a long way, in both directions
- **Let the coaches coach** and the referees ref — they're volunteers too
- **Keep it positive** — kids pick up on adult frustration faster than you'd think
- **Win or lose, shake hands** — we're teaching life skills, not just a sport

Share these with your team's parents early in the season — a short TeamSnap message before the first game sets expectations better than a mid-game correction ever will.

## Keeping Games Enjoyable for Both Sides

A lopsided game isn't fun for anyone — including the team that's winning. Ways to keep both benches in the game:

- **Spread your strongest players** across the lineup rather than stacking a line or shift
- **Rotate positions** — the kid who always plays forward learns something in goal or defense, and the score usually tightens on its own
- **When well ahead, change the goal**: require a number of passes before shooting, encourage weaker-foot/backhand attempts, move strong players to defense
- **Equal playing time is the default** — this is rec; development and fun beat winning
- **Read the other bench.** If the opposing coach is struggling with numbers or skill gaps, offer to lend a player or adjust — it's one league, not a rivalry

> **Coordinator input needed:** any sport-specific balancing rules already in use (e.g. draft/assignment practices, mercy conventions per sport) so this page reflects actual league practice, not just principles.

## When Something Goes Wrong

Any parent, player, coach, or referee issue must be reported to your sport's league mailbox (see the [home page]({{ site.baseurl }}/)) — concerns are handled by the MTL Trustees. Don't let a sideline problem fester until it boils over on a Saturday.
